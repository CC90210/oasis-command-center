# Pipeline

Audience: Client-facing

Pipeline is where interest becomes a lender-ready file. Ezra keeps leads moving, Ethan keeps applications complete, and Matt steps in for strategic calls or exceptions.

## Leads

Use Leads for merchants who are interested but not fully packaged.

- Work stages left to right: Hot Lead, Missing Info, Follow Up, Sent Application, Viewed Application, Signed Application, Submitted, Funded, Ghost, Default, Opted Out.
- Open the lead drawer for the timeline, owner details, documents, lender activity, and notes.
- Use **Send Email**, **Send SMS**, and **Call** from the drawer so the activity stays attached to the record.
- Reassign only when ownership is clear. Rep ownership affects form links, follow-up context, and visibility.
- The highest leverage action is usually getting the Full Application and 3 months of bank statements.

## Applications

Use Applications for complete or near-complete files.

- **Application In** means the deal is ready for review.
- **Missing Info** or **Requested Docs** means Ethan needs to chase the merchant before shopping.
- **Shopping** means the file is actively with lenders.
- **Funded**, **Declined**, and **Dead** are the end states that keep reporting honest.
- Re-run underwriting only when new information changes the file.

## Shopping Out

Shopping Out is the send flow for lender packages.

1. Pick the application.
2. Review the ranked lender list.
3. Confirm documents, especially statements.
4. CC the right SunBiz teammate.
5. Add a concise note when lender context matters.
6. Send to selected lenders.

If a lender is flagged for state, industry, position, or risk mismatch, do not ignore the warning. Add an override note only when Matt or the deal owner has a reason.

## Conversations

Conversations is the unified inbox for merchant messages and lender replies.

- Reply from the thread so the record stays complete.
- Use AI reply for a draft, then edit before sending.
- Open the linked lead/application before making claims about status, terms, or documents.

## Campaigns

Campaigns are for bulk SMS through TextTorrent. They are not the HTML email-template library. Use them carefully, include opt-out language, and never blast opted-out contacts.
