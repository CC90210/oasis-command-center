# Pipeline

Audience: Client-facing

Everything from a fresh prospect to a fully shopped application: your leads, the lender outreach, the deal board, your inbox, and bulk texting.

## Leads

Your prospect board — everyone who's shown interest but hasn't been shopped to lenders yet, grouped by stage.

- **Stages, left to right:** Hot Lead, Missing Info, Follow Up, Sent Application, Viewed Application, Signed Application, Submitted, Funded, Ghost, Declined, Default, Opted Out. Click a stage header to expand or collapse it.
- **New Lead** (top right) — add a prospect by hand.
- **Search box** — filter by business, contact, or email.
- **"Touch first" callout** — the single most urgent overdue lead; click **Open** to jump to it.
- **Click any lead name** to open the detail drawer with everything about them.

**Inside the lead drawer:**

- **Tabs:** Activity (full timeline), Owner (guarantor details), Lenders (shop-out results once sent), Bank (underwriting and risk flags), Docs (uploads and what's missing — a red badge means a required doc is missing), Notes (internal notes).
- **Footer actions:** **Send Email**, **Send SMS**, **Call** (rings your Kixie line then bridges to them), **Text Torrent** (enroll them in a drip sequence).
- **Assignment dropdown** — reassign the lead to a teammate.
- **Edit full record** — open the complete editable form.
- **Docs tab** — pick a document type, upload a file; uploading the last required doc can auto-advance the stage.
- **Notes tab** — type a note, then Save.

## Shopping Out

The money tab. Broadcast one application to multiple lenders in a single, professional outreach. The page walks left to right:

1. **Pick an application** — search and click one. Lenders re-rank for that deal.
2. **Choose lenders** — each has a checkbox; the **top 5 best-fit lenders are pre-checked**, and lenders that don't fit the deal's state or industry are flagged. Adjust as needed.
3. **Choose documents** — checkboxes for each file (bank statements, etc.); all on by default.
4. **CC teammates** — check Jordan or Alex to CC them, or type a Custom CC email (e.g. a processor).
5. **Add notes** (optional) — context injected into the lender email.
6. **Send to X lenders** — fires the outreach.

- **Refresh** — re-rank lenders and reload thread status.
- **High-risk send** — if a flagged lender is selected, a confirmation pops; type an override note and **Proceed Anyway** (it's logged), or **Cancel**.
- **Retry** — on any thread that errored, resets it so it re-sends.

This is where deals become money. Trust the ranking, attach the statements, CC the right people, send.

## Applications

Your deal board — full applications moving through underwriting and shopping, grouped by status.

- **Statuses:** Application In, Shopping, Missing Info, Requested Docs, Docs Out, Login, Funded, Follow Ups, Declined, Dead. Click a stage to expand or collapse.
- **New Application** — add one manually.
- **Search box** and **"Touch first"** alert, same as Leads.
- **Click any row** to open the detail drawer (same tabs as Leads: Activity, Owner, Lenders, Bank, Documents, Notes).
- **Shop Out** (drawer header) — jumps to Shopping Out with this deal pre-selected.
- **Bank tab, Re-run Underwriting** — re-grade the deal; **View full underwriting report** opens the detail page (grade, verified positions, leverage, red flags).

Underwriting usually runs on its own when the application and statements arrive. Re-run only if something changed.

## Conversations

Your unified inbox. Every SMS, call, and email with a merchant, threaded by contact.

- **Filter buttons:** All, SMS, Calls, Email.
- **Search** — find a contact by name, number, email, or message text.
- **Click a contact** to see the full thread on the right.
- **Open lead** — jump to that contact's lead.
- **Reply** — pick a provider (TextTorrent or Kixie), type in the composer, **Send**. **AI reply** drafts a suggested response you can edit before sending.
- **Recording / Transcript** links appear on calls.
